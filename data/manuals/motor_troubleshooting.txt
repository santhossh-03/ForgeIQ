INDUSTRIAL MOTOR TROUBLESHOOTING MANUAL

Machine: Industrial Electric Motor

Problem 1: Motor overheating

Possible causes:

* Excessive mechanical load.
* Blocked ventilation openings.
* Damaged bearings.
* Incorrect power supply.

Recommended inspection:

1. Check whether the motor load exceeds its rated capacity.
2. Inspect ventilation openings for dust or blockage.
3. Check bearing condition for unusual noise or vibration.
4. Have a qualified technician verify the power supply.

Problem 2: Excessive vibration

Possible causes:

* Worn or damaged bearings.
* Misalignment between the motor and connected equipment.
* Loose mounting bolts.
* Rotor imbalance.

Recommended inspection:

1. Inspect mounting bolts.
2. Check shaft and coupling alignment.
3. Examine vibration measurements against manufacturer limits.
4. Inspect bearings if vibration persists.

Problem 3: Motor misalignment

Possible causes:

* Incorrect alignment between the motor shaft and connected equipment.
* Improper installation after maintenance.
* Movement of the motor mounting base.

Possible symptoms:

* Increased vibration during operation.
* Unusual noise near the coupling.
* Increased bearing stress.

Recommended inspection:

1. Inspect shaft and coupling alignment using an appropriate method.
2. Inspect the motor mounting base for movement or looseness.
3. Compare vibration readings with manufacturer limits.
4. Have qualified maintenance personnel correct any verified misalignment.

Safety:

Disconnect and isolate electrical power before physical inspection. Follow the manufacturer's procedures and site lockout/tagout requirements. Only qualified personnel should perform electrical inspections.
